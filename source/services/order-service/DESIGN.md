# Order Service

Owns orders, immutable price/recipe snapshots, and the durable checkout saga. Checkout reserves voucher and points, applies the payable snapshot, deducts each recipe ingredient idempotently, records cash through Payment, then commits benefits. A failure before payment reverses completed stock steps and releases reservations. Once payment starts, ambiguous failures become `PAID_PENDING`: stock and benefits are never released, and the same checkout key safely resumes finalization. One loyalty point is valued at one VND and payable total must remain positive. RabbitMQ delivery is at-least-once: the relay waits for publisher confirms, the payment-event consumer deduplicates in a local inbox, retries five times, then uses a DLQ. Paid cancellation/refund behavior remains unchanged.
